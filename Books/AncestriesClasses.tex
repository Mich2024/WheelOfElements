
\section{The Ancestries and Classes}\label{Section_CharDescription}

\subsection*{Ancestries}

\begin{longtable}{|p{2.5cm}|p{13.7cm}|}
	\hline
	\textbf{Solarian} \symFire&  As creatures born of elemental  fire, they inhabit the deserts and mountains of the south. Their presence inflicts vulnerability.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Centaur}&  The noble people  of the plains blaze through enemy ranks often killing multiple at once.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Dwarf} \symEarth&  As these earthen dwellers are impervious to banes, they love getting drunk. In their strange moods, they tend to let fate decide.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Human}&  The most numerous and yet weakest species. Under the right circumstances, they are capable of mythical feats.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Silverkin} \symMetal& Metal made mortal. They are a blade that slices precise and deadly.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Half-Elf}&  With a humans ambition and decades of dedication, half-elves never truly fail. Possibility becomes certainty.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Merman} \symWater&  These mysterious dwellers of the deep open their third eye to peer into the future. They bring strange poisons and exploit opportunities.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Wyrmkin}&  Mortals that fused with the essence of dragons. %Their wounds tend to heal at unnatural rates.\\
	Their wounds close in seconds as they endure any onslaught.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Fae} \symWood&  The mischievous and helpful people of the forest are a boon to every party. They embolden their allies and let them draw out their utmost power.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Thyger}&  The cat beastmen tribe is considered the greatest of hunters for they are rarely seen and their flurries are deadly.\\
	\hline
\end{longtable}

\subsection*{Classes}

\begin{longtable}{|p{3.5cm}|p{12.7cm}|}
	
	
	\hline
	\textbf{Pyromancer} \symFire&  Blast the battlefield with AOE attacks, and stack vulnerability on your tougher targets as you amp up the heat.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Berserker} \symFire \symEarth&  Throw your cards away to obtain raw power.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Drunken Master} \symEarth&  Roll the actual dice of fate and gamble away the future of your party.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Koloss} \symEarth \symMetal&  Become nigh invulnerable.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Knight} \symMetal&  Become the vanguard. Take the advantage before the enemy can act.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Assassin} \symMetal \symWater&  Stun the enemy as you align the stars to topple even the mightiest foes.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Toxicologist} \symWater&  Heal your party as your enemies languish amidst your toxic fumes.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Sangromancer} \symWater \symWood&  Use your life as a resource to bleed yourself and the enemy dry.\\%Dance at the edge of life and death

	\textbf{Druid} \symWood&  Buff and heal your teammates as you store up your fury to devastate the enemy.\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Ranger} \symWood \symFire&  Prepare your prey with vulnerability and mow them down with dozens of small attacks.\\
	\hline
\end{longtable}

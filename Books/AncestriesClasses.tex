
\section{The Ancestries and Classes}\label{Section_CharDescription}

\subsection*{Ancestries}

\begin{longtable}{|p{2.5cm}|p{13.2cm}|p{0.5cm}|}
	\hline
	\textbf{Solarian} \symFire&  As creatures born of elemental  fire, they inhabit the deserts and mountains of the south. Their presence inflicts vulnerability. & \symVulnerable\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Centaur}&  The noble people  of the plains blaze through enemy ranks often killing multiple at once.& \symBlaze\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Dwarf} \symEarth&  As these earthen dwellers are impervious to banes, they love getting drunk. In their strange moods, they tend to let fate decide.& \symDSix\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Human}&  The most numerous and yet weakest species. Under the right circumstances, they are capable of mythical feats.& \symShield\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Silverkin} \symMetal& Metal made mortal. They are a blade that slices precise and deadly.& \symAdvantage\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Fae} &  The mischievous and helpful people of the forest are a boon to every party. They embolden their allies and let them draw out their utmost power.& \symStun\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Merman} \symWater&  These mysterious dwellers of the deep open their third eye to peer into the future. They bring strange poisons and exploit opportunities.& \symPoison\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Wyrmkin}&  Mortals that fused with the essence of dragons. %Their wounds tend to heal at unnatural rates.\\
	Their wounds close in seconds as they endure any onslaught.& \symHeal\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Half-Elf} \symWood&  With a humans ambition and decades of dedication, half-elves never truly fail. Possibility becomes certainty.& \symPowerful\\
	\hline
	\textbf{Thyger}&  The cat beastmen tribe is considered the greatest of hunters for they are rarely seen and their flurries are deadly.& \symFlurry\\
	\hline
\end{longtable}


\section{Golem Army}
Difficulty: Diamond
\subsection*{Rules}
\textbf{Rulebound Metalcrafter} - Metalcrafters cannot die while the are adjacent to a golem. They keep their damage even if the should die.
\subsection*{Story}

Some upstart warlord is trying to make an army in the northern mountain ranges. The local regent  (a Silverkin going by the name of $\epsilon$ ) has calculated that sending an adventuring army would be most cost advantageous since there is no need for the complex logistics of an army. After hearing of your considerable track record he send for your formidable party. Going up against a budding army seems like a tall task, but the regent promises that you can keep the warlords castle and the surrounding fiefdom after you are finished clearing it out. With a rise to nobility on the line you set out to quash that upstarts flame of ambition. 

You decide to strike at night in the hope of catching the opposition of guard while leveraging the small size of your party. You plan succeeds, and you catch a couple metallurges of guard while they work on a bunch of golems. You manage to strike down some of them, but the rest manage to start a couple golems in a haphazard formation.

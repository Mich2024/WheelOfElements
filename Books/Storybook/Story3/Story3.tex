\subsection*{Story}
You go to sleep on a dingy tavern, much like any other. But the sleep does not set in, no matter how hard you close our eyes. Something feels ... off. Unsure what you are even looking for, you make your way downstairs - maybe another round of drinks will finally get you tired enough.
When you arrive in the main hall, you notice a door that you cannot remember seeing before. You open the door, and you are greeting by a stairwell going down. Vague chanting accompanies you on your way down. When you finally find the next room, you are greeted by a chorus, surrounding a large stone altar with a shackled victim. Above the altar looms a priest wielding a waved ritual dagger, poised to make a sacrifice.

\subsection*{Level Up}

Determined to bring this to an end, you descend down a strange and distorted stairway, and at times you feel unsure whether or not you are even moving forward. The stench of old rotting remains fills your nostrils and your ears are ringing with an endless crescendo from the choir far below you. Your head starts throbbing as you keep hurrying down the stairs, eager to escape this liminal hell. Suddenly, you arrive into the last room where you can see a great figure created from black billowing smoke sitting on a throne.

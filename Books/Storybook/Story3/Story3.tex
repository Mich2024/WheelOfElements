
\section{Heretic Purge}
\subsection*{Rules}
\textbf{Rulebound Cultists} - When a cultist dies, put a lesser demon in its place
\subsection*{Story}
Duke Harsford has invited you to an evening masquerade on his country estate. As he has gotten up in his years, he has come to notice that some of his fellow nobles still look suspiciously handsome. You mingle with the guests and surveil them for suspicious activity. Eventually, you sweep through the garden for good measure where you interrupt two attendants during something that could be mistaken for a very intense and intimate kiss. A quick look to the neck confirms that this noble is your prey. You draw your weapons, and the masked vampire howls to call his companions.

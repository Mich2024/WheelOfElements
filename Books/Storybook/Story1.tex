
\section{Bandit Tracking}
\subsection*{Rules}
None
\subsection*{Story}
After finishing your education at the imperial academy, you decide to pursue employment as part of the imperial guard.
The pay is steady, but the job contains a *lot* more paperwork and minor infractions than you expected, since the roads closest to the capital are well patroled.
One day you stumble across a broken carriages, dead horses and the stench of blood hanging thick over the road.
Some bandits are still looting the caravan. It is time to apply your training.


\section{Combat}
Most of your time will be spent in combat. During combat, your choices during levelling up and deckbuilding are tested against challenging foes.
\subsection*{Start} 
When entering an encounter from the Storybook, perform this setup once:\\
\begin{minipage}[t][2.7cm][t]{\textwidth}
	\begin{itemize}
		
		\item \textbf{Action Deck} - Shuffle your Action deck 
		\item \textbf{Elemental Wheel} - Empty it
		\item \textbf{Life} - Set your life to starting life. Your starting life is all Classes and Ancestry life added together.
		\item \textbf{Enemies} - Setup enemies based on the Storybook.
		\item \textbf{Passives} - Start of combat passives trigger
		
	\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}


\textbf{Enemy setup} - The enemy placement is given by the encounter in the storybook. The order in which enemies are placed cannot be changed without an explicit action allowing you to do so. The first player is designated by the players. All others follow clockwise as they are seated. This seating order determines which players gets which enemies.

\textbf{Turn order} - The first turn goes to the players, followed by an enemy turn. Alternate between player and enemy turn until either every enemy or player is defeated.

\subsection*{End}
When  \textbf{all enemies are defeated} in one encounter, you  \textbf{win} the encounter. If all encounters are won, you complete the story successfully.
If all players are downed at the same time your journey ends, and you lose the entire story. In case only some  \textbf{players are downed}, but the encounter is won, those players incur no penalty,  \textbf{return to full life} and win together with the party.

\begin{tcolorbox}[colframe=black, colback=white]
	When an enemy is defeated, immediately put that enemy back into the box.
\end{tcolorbox}


\subsection*{Player Turn}

\textbf{Simultaneous Turns} - All players play at the  \textbf{same time}. This means you do not have to wait for another player when playing your cards. You  \textbf{can coordinate} with other players to sequence cards in your favour.
\begin{tcolorbox}[colframe=black, colback=white]
	In simpler situations its more enjoyable to play cards, instead of discussing.
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Monster Die} - Roll the Monster Die
	\item \textbf{Draw} - Draw up to your maximum hand size.The default maximum hand size is 4. If your draw pile is empty, shuffle it\\
	\item \textbf{Passives} - Start of turn passives trigger. Players may decide order
	\item \textbf{Play} - Players may play up to 2 cards each turn \textit{(See Page \pageref{Section_PlayingACard})}.
	To play a card:\\ 
	\hspace*{1cm} \textbf{Pay} any element cost you must or want\\
	\hspace*{1cm} \textbf{Perform} all card actions you can perform\\
	\hspace*{1cm} \textbf{Discard} the played card into the discard pile, keep all unplayed cards in your hand\\
	\hspace*{1cm} \textbf{Downed} if your life pool is at 0, you are downed, and you can only play a single card each turn.\\
	\item \textbf{Retain} your other cards in your hand
\end{itemize}

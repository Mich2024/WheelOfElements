Card symbols.
\begin{longtable}{|p{2.5cm}|p{13.7cm}|}
	\hline
	Attack X \symAttack & \textbf{Target one enemy, roll your attack dice} and sum the result.\\
	\hline
	Heal X \symHeal & \textbf{Restore X health} to the target.\\
	\hline
	Blaze X \symBlaze & If played as the  \textbf{first card} per turn ->  \textbf{Discard X card at random} and gain the effect below.\\
	\hline
	Lifepay \symLife & Pay \textbf{life instead of elements}. You can still play those cards while backlined.\\
	\hline
	Ranged \symRanged & \textbf{Target your neighbours} or their enemies. \\
	\hline
	Elements X \symFire \symEarth \symMetal \symWater \symWood &\textbf{Add X element markers} to field of that element. \\
	\hline
	Disengage \symDisengage &  \textbf{Rotate your target} clockwise to the next player.\\
	\hline 
	Area Of Effect \centering{\includegraphics[width=2cm]{../../Pictures/AOE/AoeRuleSample.png}} & \textbf{Target enemies in the exact pattern} apply the full card to each target.\\
	\hline
	Powerful \symPowerful & Your \symAttack~attacks, \symPoison~poisons, and \symHeal~heals are  \textbf{increased by one}. \\
	\hline
	Advantage \symAdvantage & Spend one advantage to \textbf{re-roll one attack die}.\\
	\hline
	Shield \symShield & When taking damage from an \symAttack~attack or \symPoison~poison, \textbf{take one less}.\\
	\hline
	Flurry \symFlurry & Spend one flurry token to perform \symAttack:1. \\
	\hline
	Poison X \symPoison & During the Boon \& Bane phase, \textbf{lose X life}.\\
	\hline
	Vulnerable X \symVulnerable & Every time you take damage \textbf{take X extra}.\\
	\hline
	Stun \symStun & \textbf{Players} - When playing a card while stunned, instead \textbf{ignore all of the cards effects and remove one stun marker}.\\
	\hline
\end{longtable}

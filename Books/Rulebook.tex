\textbf{Game Overview}\\
Wheel of Elements is a cooperative multi-classing deckbuilder. Become an indomitable tank, a Mage of Immense arcane Power or something between, like a berserking Alchemist. Beat three battles of increasing difficulty and prove your mettle.\\

Wheel of Elements is a \textbf{multi-classing} game. Each player starts with a Race and two classes. Improve or add a class after each won combat.\\

Wheel of Elements is also a \textbf{deckbuilder}. Each Race and class gives you cards which combine to a 15 card Deck. Many cards twist the rules and let you get creative with your combinations., Choose the way in which you create your deck.\\ 

\textbf{Playtime}\\
The Game consists of three fights with random events and deckbuilding sessions between them. A full game takes about 2 hours. Plan for some extra time your first game.\\

\textbf{Winning \& Losing}\\
Victory - Victory is achieved when all enemies are dead.\\
Loss - You lose when every player dies in the same fight.\\

\section{Setup}
\begin{enumerate}	
	\item Pick a Race
	\item Pick a Level 1 Class
	\item Pick a different level 1 Class
	\item Shuffle your Action decks together
	\item Do the modifier changes to your modifier Deck depending of the Race Tableau and shuffle it. Then put the class shingles in the Race Tableau
	\item Put the Elemental Wheel in the middle
	\item Select a story from the storybook
	\item Each player setups the his enemies corresponding to his Fight 1 encounter card
	\item Start Combat
\end{enumerate}

\section{Play Area}

Action draw pile - Shuffle your deck before combat begins to create your draw pile
Action discard pile - After you play a card, it goes here
Modifier draw pile - Shuffle your deck before combat begins to create your draw pile
Modifier discard pile - After you modify an attack, all drawn modifiers go here
Tableau - The tableau tracks you level up decision and life
Enemy Zone - Enemies go here, with the configuration described in the storybook


\section{Your Decks}
\subsection{The action Deck}
Card pool - The cards that can be added to your deck
Before combat select 15 cards, these will be your deck
The action deck contains all cards you can play during combat.
Card rarity - Cards can have different rarity levels, from bronze to mithril.
Card power varies greatly, but two cards of the same rarity are generally equal

\subsection{The modifier Deck}
The modifier cards are used every time you attack an enemy. They can have three effects:
Numerical - Adds a number to your attack damage
Elements - Create the shown elements
Draw - Perform all other effects of this modifier, and draw another


\section{Leveling Up}

When you level up, you may choose a new class and add its shingle to your tableau.
The class shingles have to be placed in the leftmost slot of their rank.
Rank 1 classes go in the top row.
Rank 2 classes go in the middle row.
Rank 3 classes go in the bottom. %Picture for this
Each slot for a shingle has changes to the modifier deck printed on it.
When you cover a slot with a shingle, add or remove the printed modifiers to your modifier deck.
Add the chosen classes cards to your card pool.
Increase your life tracker to reflect the new maximum life.

\section{The Storybook}
The storybook contains all of the Stories you can play in Wheel of Elements.
Each story will take about 2 hours to play through, contain 3 Encounters and one text containing a short story for why you are fighting.

\subsection{Stories}
Each story begins with a plot hook, which should be narrated by a player.
	The text is separated into four sections. After reading a section, start one of the three fights. After finishing a fight, read another section, draw an event, and level up. After finishing the second fight, repeat this process.

\section{Combat Round}
\subsection{Player Turn}
Shuffle your Action deck and your modifier deck before the combat starts. Empty the Elemental Wheel.\\
Start of Turn\\
Draw up to your maximum hand size.
	Empty draw pile - When your draw pile is empty and you need to draw, your discard pile becomes your new draw pile. Shuffle it.
Roll the Monster Die
Start of turn and start of combat abilities trigger. Players may decide order.
Play\\
Players may play up to 2 cards each turn.
To play a card:
Pay its mandatory element cost
Choose if you want to play the optional costs.
Resolve the card.
Put the played card into the discard pile.

\subsection{Enemy Turn}
Your leftmost enemy, that didnt act yet, uses their action. 
Resolve the action. 
Repeat until all your enemies acted, even if you die.
	
\subsection{Cleanup step}
Remove all single Turn Boons\&Banes (They are Shield, Player Powerful, Advantage etc).\\
Apply Poison Damage.\\
Move your leftmost enemy to the player to your left. This happens simultaneous. You cannot give away an enemy given to you this way.\\
If you are dead give all your remaining enemies to your left. \\
Rotate the mana wheel.\\
Shuffle the modifier discard back into the deck if the *0 or *2 modifieris in the discard.

\section{Playing a Card}
You may play two cards per turn. Most cards do not have a cost. Others have mana costs.
To pay  a mana cost, remove the stated amount of mana markers from the corresponding field on the wheel back into the stash.
If the cost is not optional, you may not play it without paying the mana cost.

When playing a card, you execute all effects from top to bottom.
All effects have to be used if possible.

The most common effects are attacks and mana generation.
To perform an attack, first select an enemy to attack.
Secondly, you reveal a modifier from you modifer deck, and add its value to the attack.
Every Deck also has a *2 and a *0. In that case, multiply your attak value by two (or  zero) after everything else was applied.

To generate elemental mana, check how many mana symbols are shown on the card, and move that many markers from the stash to the wheel, on the corresponding field.

If the card had an optional cost, some actions are separated below by a line.
Only perform these actions if the optional cost is paid.

Some cards have an Area Of Effect (AOE) marker. If so, target monsters in the pattern shown by the AOE.
Then repeat all attacks and banes for each monster that is targeted by the AOE.

After playing the card, put it into your discard pile

\section{Optional Rules}
When you become more experienced, you can start with one class at rank two, instead of two classes at rank 1

If you find it very hectic for all players to take the turns simultaneously, you can choose to play one player at a time. In this case it may be nice for all players to look at the current players hand and discuss.

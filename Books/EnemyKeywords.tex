
\subsection*{Enemy Keywords}
Enemies can have passives too. They are abbreviated with keywords.

\begin{longtable}{|p{2.5cm}|p{13.7cm}|}
	
	\hline
	Skittish & This enemy \symDisengage disengages immediately after being damaged (You still perform the full action before the enemy disengages) \\
	Harrier & This enemy \symDisengage disengages at the end of every enemy turn\\
	Heretic & This enemy rotates to the right instead of left during cleanup (does not apply when disengaged)\\
	Enrage & This enemy gains \symPowerful Powerful after taking damage (also triggers from poison)\\
	Scenariobound & This enemy has extra rules which are explained in the scenario\\
	\hline 
	
\end{longtable}

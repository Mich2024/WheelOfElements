		\textbf{Game Overview}\\
		Wheel of Elements is a cooperative Card Battler and Deckbuilder for 3 to 5 players. Sculpt your deck, find unexpected synergies, fight hordes of enemies and see them dance around the wheel.\\
		
		Wheel of Elements is a \textbf{Deckbuilder}. Over the course of the game you will branch into \textbf{multiple classes} letting you add and remove cards from your deck. Many cards synergize, letting you get creative with your combinations. \\
		
		Your \textbf{battles} revolve around the \textbf{Wheel of Elements}. Coordinate with the players to your left and right when taking down threats, and work as a team when generating and using elements. \\
		
		\textbf{Playtime}\\
		The game consists of three fights with random events and deckbuilding sessions between them. A full game takes about 2.5 hours. Plan in some extra time for your first game.\\
		
		\textbf{Winning \& Losing}\\
		\textbf{Victory} - Victory is achieved when all enemies from the story are defeated.\\
		\textbf{Loss} - You lose when every player is downed during one of the fights.\\

			\textbf{Rule 0:} The game is about having fun. If you and your playgroup decide that some change to the rules would increase your fun, experiment with it!

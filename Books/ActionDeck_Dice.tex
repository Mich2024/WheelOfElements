\section{The Action Deck}
%Dezenter reminder über das level up mit Seitenzahl zu level up segment
The Action Deck contains all action cards you can draw and subsequently play during combat.\\
As a deckbuilding game the action deck will continue to evolve during gameplay. \textit{See Page \pageref{Section_CharacterBuilding}}\\
Your first action deck is composed of all of the action cards from your ancestry, and your two classes. 

\textbf{Deckbuilding} Your action deck consists of exactly \textbf{16 cards}. When you put new cards in, you must taken an equal amount out. You keep all cards you acquire at your disposal, and you may return them to your deck later - but some other cards will have to go if you choose to put the old ones in.

\section{The Ancestry Dice}
These dice modify the results of your \symAttack attacks.
Every time you pick a class, you will get an additional ancestry die. You always use all dice at once.
The ancestry dice can have four effects. Some sides are blank and have no effect. Each die also has one side with a reminder of the ancestry it belongs to

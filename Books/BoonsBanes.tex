\subsection*{Static Keywords}
These keywords provide a singular effect or modify how an action is performed.
\begin{longtable}{|p{2.5cm}|p{13.7cm}|}
	\hline
	Heal X \symHeal & Restore X health to the target. This restoration can put the target above its starting health.\\
	\hline
	Blaze X \symBlaze & If you play a card with blaze as the first card of your tun, discard X cards, and perform the optional effect. If you play that card second, do not discard and do not perform the optional text. \\
	\hline
	D6 \symDSix & When a card asks you to roll some D6, get that many six sided dice, roll them and add their results. Most often, you attack for an amount of D6. Sometimes the result is used as a variable X and will be referenced on the card. The D6 is not part of your ancestry dice, and cannot be re-rolled with advantage.\\
	\hline
	Ranged \symRanged & All actions after the Ranged keyword may target your neighbours or their enemies. \\
	\hline
	Elements \symFire \symEarth \symMetal \symWater \symWood& If element generation is part of your cards actions, add that many element markers to field of the shown element on the inner part of the wheel. \\
	\hline
	Disengage \symDisengage & To disengage an enemy, rotate that enemy to the left. This rotation does not respect enemy order or the end of turn, and happens immediately. \\
	\hline 
	Area Of Effect \centering{\includegraphics[width=2cm]{../Pictures/AOE/AoeRuleSample.png}} & Some cards have AOE emblems. In this case, target enemies in the exact pattern shown in the pictogram. AOEs can never affect enemies from multiple different players. If you have less enemies than AOE targets, the additional targets go unused.\\
	\hline
	AOE All \centering{\includegraphics[height=1.5cm]{../Pictures/AOE/AoeAll.png}} & A special case of AOE emblem. If your card has this, target all of your enemies at once. \\
	\hline
\end{longtable}

\subsection*{Status Boons \& Banes}
\textbf{Tracking} - Those types of Boons \& Banes are tracked with status tokens. When you apply a status boon or bane to a player or an enemy, place a status token on it.

\begin{tcolorbox}[colframe=black, colback=white]
	All status tokens can stack, which results in the effect being applied multiple times.
	For example, if powerful is applied three times on you, place three powerful tokens on your Ancestry Tableau and your attacks now deal three extra damage.
\end{tcolorbox}

\textbf{Boons} 
\begin{longtable}{|p{2.5cm}|p{13.7cm}|}
	
	\hline
	Powerful \symPowerful & Your attacks deal one extra damage. Remove powerful from players at the end of the turn. For enemies, powerful persists between turns. \\
	\hline
	Advantage \symAdvantage & After rolling your ancestry dice, you may re-roll a number of them equal or less to your Advantage stacks. Re-roll them all at once. Do this only once each time you roll your ancestry dice. Remove Advantage from players at the end of the turn. Enemies cannot gain advantage.\\
	\hline
	Shield \symShield & When taking damage from an \symAttack attack (not self inflicted or poison), take one less. Remove shield from players at the end of the turn. For enemies shield persists between turns. \\
	\hline
	Flurry \symFlurry & At any point during your turn, you may spend one flurry token to perform \symAttack:1. This causes you to roll ancestry and grants any benefit attached to a normal attack. Flurry persists between turns on both players and enemies. \\
	\hline
		
\end{longtable}

\textbf{Banes} 

\begin{longtable}{|p{2.5cm}|p{13.7cm}|}
	
	\hline
	Poison \symPoison & At the end of the turn, lose one life. Poison persists between turns on both players and enemies.\\
	\hline
	Vulnerable \symVulnerable & When taking damage from an attack, take one additional damage. Some cards let you deal double damage. The extra damage from vulnerable is multiplied too. Vulnerable persists between turns on both players and enemies. \\
	\hline
	Stun \symStun & \textbf{Players} - When playing a card while stunned, instead ignore all of the cards effects and remove one stun marker. You still have to pay mandatory element costs if the card had them. \hspace*{12cm} \linebreak \textbf{Enemies} - When an enemy is stunned they instead skip their full action to remove one stun token. Stun persists between turns. They move in the Clean up phase nevertheless.\\
	\hline
	
	
	
\end{longtable}


\begin{tcolorbox}[colframe=black, colback=white]
	Poison and Vulnerable can become very painful in longer fights since they cannot be healed. 
\end{tcolorbox}

\section{Character Building}
\label{Section_CharacterBuilding}

After completing an encounter end an event, your character has experienced a lot, and is ready to level up.

\textbf{Ancestry} - 
In \textit{Wheel of Elements} there are 11 different ancestries to choose from. The ancestries are dedicated to \textbf{one Boon or Bane} and often also to an element. Try experimenting to see which playstyles you like. Each ancestry comes with 7 ancestry dice, one passive, 6 action cards and a tableau.

\textbf{Ancestry Tableau} - your core tool for tracking character progress.
It contains the ancestry name, places for all of the class tiles, your first passive, a place to track your life, and an overview of the combat turn.


\textbf{Attack Dice} - Whenever you get a class tile, it will cover a square on your ancestry tableau.
Each filled tile slot adds an ancestry dice.
When you cover a slot with a tile, add the printed Attack Dice to your dice pool.


\textbf{Placement} - The class tiles are placed in the ancestry tableau in the \textbf{leftmost open slot of their rank}.
Rank 1 classes go in the top row,
rank 2 classes in the middle row, and
rank 3 classes in the bottom row.
To begin the game, you will choose two rank 1 classes.
The respective Class tiles are always placed at the top left in the rectangle.

\textbf{Passives} - The passives you acquire will shape your gameplay immensely. Passives come in three varieties: 

\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Trigger} - These passives apply at specific times, like the start of combat, or the end of your turn. You are granted a concrete benefit at the specified time
	\item \textbf{Permanent} - Some passives modify the way you perform actions permanently. For example, you might gain the ability to use your ancestry dice when applying certain Boons or Banes
	\item \textbf{Shaping} - Some passives modify what you would normally get from an ancestry or class. They might provide additional ancestry dice, more powerful cards, or simply more maximum life
\end{itemize}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colframe=black, colback=white]
	The tableau provides a helpful overview of all of the passives that affect you.
\end{tcolorbox}


\textbf{Leveling Up} - After the second and third event, you level up.
Rank 1 classes can be taken without requirements. You may \textbf{choose any one class you like}, regardless of ancestry. Rank 2 classes require you to already have the same class on rank 1. Rank 3 classes require having the rank 2 class. Slot the new Class Tile into your tableau, take the cards of that class, and increase your starting life by the amount stated on the new class. You always add new classes, and you never loose passives, life, dice or cards you have gained before.

\textbf{Deckbuilding} - After levelling up, you also have new action cards at your disposal. You may add the new ones (or old ones you removed) to your deck. When you add a card, also remove one to \textbf{keep your deck at a size of 16 cards}. Shuffle before starting the encounter.

\begin{tcolorbox}[colframe=black, colback=white]
	Every class comes with one passive, 5 cards and around 3 starting life.
\end{tcolorbox}

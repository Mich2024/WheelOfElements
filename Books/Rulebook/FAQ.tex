\section{FAQ}


\begin{tcolorbox}[colframe=blue, colback=white]
	Reminder - Reading the card explains the card -- except when you have to look in here.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection*{General}
\begin{enumerate}
	\item If I disengage all enemies in front of me, but there are still actions on the card, can I use the actions at range?\\
	\hspace*{1cm}- Yes.
	\item When I end my turn and flip the leftmost enemy sideways, does that enemy behave differently?\\
	\hspace*{1cm}- No, being sideways is just an indicator.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Ancestries}

\subsubsection*{Solarian}
\begin{enumerate}
	\item Does my passive still work when backlined?\\
	\hspace*{1cm}- No, enemies just pass you by.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection*{Merman}
\begin{enumerate}
	\item How does my passive interact with the assassin 2 passive?\\
	\hspace*{1cm}- Badly, as a merman you first roll your dice, then you reroll your dice from advantage and only then you gain your advantage.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection*{Fae}
\begin{enumerate}
	\item How does \symRanged Ranged \symFlurry Flurry work?\\
	\hspace*{1cm}- You can give the flurry tokens to someone else. Spending a flurry token does not benefit from your passive.
\end{enumerate}
\subsection*{Classes}

\subsubsection*{Sangromancer}
\begin{enumerate}
	\item Can I send myself to the backline by paying life until I hit 0?\\
	\hspace*{1cm}- Yes.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection*{Bard}
\begin{enumerate}
	\item Can I heal backlined players for my Bard 2 passive?\\
	\hspace*{1cm}- Yes.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Enemies}
\subsubsection*{Bandit Leader}
\begin{enumerate}
	\item How does stun work with his extra action?\\
	\hspace*{1cm}- Stunning he bandit leader will remove the bonus action from his adjacent bandits. Stunning an adjacent bandit will remove only one of that bandits actions.
\end{enumerate}

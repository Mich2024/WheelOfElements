
\section{Combat}
Most of your time will be spent in combat. During combat, your choices during leveling up and deckbuilding are tested against challenging foes.
\subsection*{Start} 
When entering an encounter from the Storybook, perform this setup once:\\
\begin{minipage}[t][2.7cm][t]{\textwidth}
	\begin{itemize}
		
		\item \textbf{Action Deck} - Shuffle your Action deck 
		\item \textbf{Wheel of Elements} - Remove all element markers from the wheel
		\item \textbf{Life} - Set your life to starting life. Your starting life is all Classes and Ancestries life added together
		\item \textbf{Enemies} - Setup enemies based on the Storybook
		\item \textbf{Passives} - Start-of-combat passives trigger
		
	\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}


\textbf{Enemy setup} - The enemy placement is given by the encounter in the storybook. The order in which enemies are placed cannot be changed without an explicit action allowing you to do so. Player number 1 is designated by the players. All others follow clockwise as they are seated. This seating order determines which players gets which enemies. You do not need to put much thought into this, as the order is used just for setup and all players face roughly equal difficulty of enemies.

\textbf{Turn order} - The first turn goes to the players, followed by an enemy turn. The player take their turn simultaneously. When every player finishes playing cards, synchronise and move to the enemy turn. All players can then perform the actions of their enemies simultaneously. Alternate between player and enemy turn until either every enemy is defeated or every player is backlined.

\subsection*{End}
When  \textbf{all enemies are defeated} in one encounter, you  \textbf{win} the encounter. If all three encounters are won, you complete the story successfully.
If all players are backlined at the same time your journey ends, and you lose the entire story. In case only some  \textbf{players are backlined}, but an \textbf{encounter is won}, those players incur no penalty and win together with the party. If the players won, all players \textbf{return to starting life} for the next encounter.

\begin{tcolorbox}[colframe=black, colback=white]
	When an enemy is defeated, immediately put that enemy back into the box.
\end{tcolorbox}


\subsection*{Player Turn}

\textbf{Simultaneous Turns} - All players play at the  \textbf{same time}. This means you do not have to wait for another player when playing your cards. You  \textbf{can coordinate} with other players to sequence cards in your favour.
\begin{tcolorbox}[colframe=black, colback=white]
	In simpler situations, it is more enjoyable to just play cards instead of discussing.
\end{tcolorbox}

\textbf{Turn order}:
\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Enemy Die} - Roll the Enemy Die \textit{(See Enemy Turn, Page \pageref{Section_EnemyTurn})}
	\item \textbf{Draw} - Draw up to your maximum hand size. The default maximum hand size is 4. When your draw pile goes empty, shuffle your discard pile, and it becomes the new draw pile
	\item \textbf{Passives} - Start-of-turn passives trigger. Players may decide the trigger order
	\item \textbf{Play} - Players may play up to 2 cards each turn \textit{(See Playing a Card, Page \pageref{Section_PlayingACard})}.
	To play a card:\\ 
	\hspace*{1cm} \textbf{Pay} any mandatory, and if desired optional, element cost\\
	\hspace*{1cm} \textbf{Perform} all actions you can perform\\
	\hspace*{1cm} \textbf{Discard} the played card into the discard pile
	\item \textbf{Retain} your unplayed cards in your hand
\end{itemize}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colframe=black, colback=white]
	\textbf{Backlined} - If your life pool is at 0, you are backlined, and you can only play a single card each turn. Your health will be reset for the next encounter, but you cannot rise above 0 for the remainder of the current encounter\\
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection*{Beginner Combinations} 

For your first time playing, the sheer amount of options can be overwhelming. We recommend these options:\\
\begin{minipage}[t][7cm][t]{1\textwidth}
\begin{enumerate}
	\item \textbf{Human, Knight, Koloss} - Human grants you the strongest card in the game, "Smite". The knight passive allows you to see half your deck on turn 1 to execute a devastating opening strike. Koloss keeps you alive. Level into more ranks of Koloss for survivability, or rank up Knight to improve your first turn.
	\item \textbf{Half Elf, Ranger, Bard} - This very potent combination lets you deal high amounts of damage. Half Elf and Bard improve each attack, while Ranger lets you hit with many small attacks. Choose Ranger ranks when leveling up to increase your damage further. Bard is for a more supportive playstyle.
	\item \textbf{Fae, Druid, Assassin} - Fae allows you to play every card as if it had ranged. Druid and Assassin let you heal your allies and stun your enemies. This combines to let you shape the flow of battle and keep your teammates alive. Choosing more ranks of Assassin improves your damage, while more ranks of Druid let you support better.
	\item \textbf{Dwarf, Berserker, Pyromancer} - Dwarves are naturally sturdy, which complements the damage-focussed Pyromancer and Berserker for a well rounded character.  Both Pyromancer and Berserker will offer more damage as you get higher ranks. 
	\item \textbf{Centaur, Sangromancer, Witch} - Centaurs let you use the damage that is usually wasted when finishing an enemy by redirecting it to an adjacent enemy. This combines well with the high damage output of Sangromancer. The Witch's passive is used to heal back the life costs of Sangromancer cards. Sangromancer provides more damage when leveling up, so that path is recommended. 
\end{enumerate}
\end{minipage}

All  ancestries. classes, elements and mechanics are arranged into one large wheel.
Classes that are placed adjacent to each other will share their elements, mechanics and synergies.
